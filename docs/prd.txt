Product  Name  
AFRIBN  (Africa  Bureau  of  News)  
Product  Vision  
AFRIBN  is  Africa's  Intelligence  Operating  System.  
The  platform  transforms  fragmented  information  from  news,  government  publications,  policy  
documents,

Problem  Statement  
Decision-makers  operating  across  Africa  face  several  challenges:  
●  Information  is  fragmented  across  thousands  of  sources.  ●  High-impact  developments  are  often  buried  in  low-value  news.  ●  Verification  is  difficult.  ●  Intelligence  arrives  too  late.  ●  There  is  no  unified  platform  connecting  policies,  deals,  risks,  events,  and  field  
intelligence.
 
Organizations  need:  
●  Real-time  situational  awareness.  ●  Verified  intelligence.  ●  Early  warning  signals.  ●  Strategic  context.  ●  Actionable  recommendations.

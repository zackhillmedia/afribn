AFRIBN — the Africa Bureau of News — is a strategic intelligence network turning the continent's news into decision-grade insight for the people who shape it.

Our mission

Decision-grade intelligence, not headlines

Africa is the world's fastest-changing strategic landscape — yet the people making the biggest decisions about it often work from fragmented headlines, stale reports and unverified rumor. AFRIBN exists to close that gap.

We built an intelligence production system, not a news feed. Every item moves through source-reliability scoring, corroboration, analyst review, transparent scoring, field verification and editorial sign-off before it reaches a single decision-maker. The result is intelligence you can act on — and defend.
